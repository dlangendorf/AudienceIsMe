# What is MyMedical Base?

This is my "elevator pitch" for MyMedical Base.

**What is MyMedical Base?** For now it's just an idea -- for an app, a service, a physical journal (TBD) -- and using Audience Is Me is where I begin to define this . . . or at least a place to jot down some thoughts, brainstorm, and make sense of what whatever I'm thinking.

So, what am I thinking?

My . . . Medical . . . Base.

**My** as in it's mine, focused on me.

**Medical** because medical is what this all about. Anything and everything medical in a person's life.

**Base** because this, at the moment, resembles a database (hence the *base*), but without all the technical complexity.

Adding it up, **MyMedical Base is a personal, secure, and simple way to record, tag, file away, retrieve and use all the medical information that's important in someone's life.** A medical dashboard of sorts that is readily available, convenient to use, user customizable, and gives people "peace of mind" knowing their medical information will be there when needed.

That's it.

## One More Thought

**Could this be an app?** Sure, if I knew how to code and develop software. The most likely outcome for MyMedicalBase is, in fact, an app. But what kind of app? How can I develop without coding experience? Questions for JKyle and Chip.

**Could this be a service?** Probably, but I haven't gone too far down this rabbit hole. It could be a dumb-ass idea.

**Could this be a physical product?** Yes. Or more likely a combination medical app with a physical journal companion (or even a book!).
