## Welcome

What is Audience Is Me?

Audience Is Me is a place for me to write, research, record, comment, play, explore, practice writing (I'm a little rusty).

Why just me?

For the moment, I want to explore and play. I don't want to build an audience. I don't want to be involved in social media and social networks. I don't want to rate, like, amass viewers. I don't want to mess with subscriptions, newsletters or compete in today's blogosphere, or whatever the content "creator-sphere" is spewing these days.

At least for now.

Maybe later when I develop some worthy content.

I  have project ideas in mind and Audience Is Me is a good place to work on them, creating brain dumps, briefs, visualizations, and documentation.

At this time, Audience Is Me is a place to . . . 

- **make sense of all the medical stuff I've been doing for the last 10+ years** . . . working with dad, mom, and my own medical issues; from this I "built" my own "**Medical Base**" using Obsidian ⬇️
- (after using my "Medical Base" for nearly a year I see opportunity for something . . . maybe an app . . . maybe a service . . . but whatever this is it will require additional research and understanding) ⬇️
- **define what this opportunity is; I am temporarily calling it MyMedical Base** -- "my" for me or personal, "medical" for all things medical (health) in my life, and "base" because, for now, what results is a personal medical "database" ⬇️
- from my "Medical Base" I developed the **Daily Health Report** and the **Daily Health Log**, which led to the creation "**MyMedical Base**" (working name) using Obsidian and the plugin "Bases"
- (⬆️ information used in the Daily Health Report and Medical Base is derived from consumer health devices like an Apple Watch, an OMRON blood pressure cuff, a smart scale, consolidated in the Apple Health app and extracted into Obsidian via [health.md](https://healthmd.app) ⬇️ 
- **write about consumer health technology** ⬆️; I've been working with this stuff for more than 10 years, even longer if I include the chest straps, watches, and sensors I used when cycling years ago
- **write about medical caregiving, my own experiences, getting older, maybe end of life** . . .

If all goes well, the information compiled here may be of interest to some, but first I have to figure out what "this" is.
