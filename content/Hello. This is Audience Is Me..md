## Welcome, Me

**Edit**: might as well add a line for when I update a note, which is what I am doing now.

No idea what Audience Is Me is going to be, but it's a start.

Audience Is Me is a site for me. Hence the name. I'm writing for myself. I have a project or two or three in mind, which may be of interest to some . . . but that's for later, not now.

Audience Is Me is a place to . . . 

- **make sense of all the medical stuff I've been doing** for the last 10 years or more . . . working with dad, mom, my own medical issues
- **to define what I am temporarily calling MyMedical Base**, which could eventually become an app . . . but more of that later . . . 
- to introduce the **Daily Health Report** and the **Daily Health Log** and explain how these work . . . 
- to write about, or comment on, **consumer health technology**; I've been working with this stuff as an aging consumer for more than 10 years, even longer if I include the chest straps, watches, and sensors I used when bike riding 10 to 20 years ago.

I am sure there are plenty of sources like AARP and a bunch of websites, social network sites, blogs, Instas, Tik Toks, YouTube videos with similar content, but I don't want to repeat that content.

This is for me.
