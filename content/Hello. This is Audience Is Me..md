## Welcome

So far the only visitors are me. Hello, Me.

Here are a few initial thoughts:

No idea what Audience Is Me is going to be, but it's a start.

Audience Is Me is a site for me. Hence the name. I'm writing for myself. I have a project or two or three in mind, which may be of interest to some . . . but that's for later, not now. 

At this time, Audience Is Me is a place to . . . 

- **make sense of all the medical stuff I've been doing** for the last 10 years or more . . . working with dad, mom, my own medical issues
- **define what I am temporarily calling MyMedical Base**, which could eventually become an app . . . but more of that later . . . 
- **introduce the Daily Health Report** and the **Daily Health Log** and explain how these work . . . 
- **write about consumer health technology**; I've been working with this stuff as an aging consumer for more than 10 years, even longer if I include the chest straps, watches, and sensors I used when cycling 10 to 20 years ago.
- **write about **medical caregiving, my own experiences, getting, and end of life** . . . but not now.

**Warning**:
I probably will include other interests that people, should this escape into the wild, won't care about but I do -- so be it.

In no order, soccer/football (St. Louis CITY), baseball (St. Louis Cardinals), music, jazz, books, science, dogs, movies, TV shows, food, art, science, tech, medical, and so on and on. I'm fascinated by it all, but I will stay away from politics and cultural bullshit because I want this to be a happy place, a safe place, not a doomscrolling crack den or dungeon (nod to Carl).

This is for me.
