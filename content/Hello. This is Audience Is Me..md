## Welcome, Me

No idea what this is going to be, but it's a start. Audience is Me is:

- **a place to work on MyMedical Base** (working title), which could be an app or something else entirely; I'll play with the idea here and maybe share it with a friend or two for input
- **a place to document the whole Medical and Medical Base thing I'm doing**, which includes the **Daily Health Report** (via health.md) and the **Daily Health Log**
- in a more bird's eye view, **this is a place to document, comment on, or write whatever I feel like writing about aging, my own health struggles, Dad's, Mom's** . . . and so on and so forth
- in a more bird's eye view, **a place to write about consumer health** technology and my experiences using Apple Watches, digital scales, blood pressure cuffs, health apps . . . all of it.

For now this is private. Depending on what happens, I may keep this totally private or begin to open up certain sections because some of the information may be of interest or value to someone.

Oh, yeah, I forgot: I'll probably include interests, which may not interest anybody else . . . soccer/football (St. Louis CITY, MLS, Real Madrid, Earling Haaland, the World Cup), music, jazz, books, science, dogs, movies, tv shows, food, art . . . so many interests. I plan to stay away from politics and the cultural bullshit that's going because I want this to be a happy place, not one more doomscrolling crack den.

More than anything, I need to write again. It's been too long, but I don't want to start another blog, or to write some influencer specialty blog of some sort, or create social media content. I just want a place to go, to experiment, to pontificate, to be stupid, to explore, to not add to the noise.
