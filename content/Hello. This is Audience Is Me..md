## Welcome

What is Audience Is Me?

Audience Is Me is a place for me to write, research, record, comment, play, explore, practice writing (I'm a little rusty).

Why just me?

For the moment, I want to explore and play. I don't want to build an audience. I don't want to be involved in social media and social networks. I don't want to rate, like, engage. I don't want to mess with subscriptions, newsletters or to compete in today's blogosphere, or whatever content "creator-sphere" is spewing these days.

I do have some project ideas in mind and Audience Is Me is a good place to work on them, create briefs, research, document,  which may be of interest to some. The app idea floating around in my head I think would be of interest, but I have more to do to figure that out.

At this time, Audience Is Me is a place to . . . 

- **make sense of all the medical stuff I've been doing** for the last 10 years or more . . . working with dad, mom, my own medical issues
- **define what I am temporarily calling MyMedical Base**, which could eventually become an app . . . but more on that later . . . 
- **introduce the Daily Health Report** and the **Daily Health Log** and explain how these work . . . This is the foundation of what is becoming MyMedical Base.
- **write about consumer health technology**; I've been working with this stuff as an aging consumer for more than 10 years, even longer if I include the chest straps, watches, and sensors I used when cycling 10 to 20 years ago.
- **write about medical caregiving, my own experiences, getting older, and end of life** . . . but not now.

**Warning**:
I will include other interests that many people, should this escape into the wild, won't care about but I do -- so be it. They're all interesting. I promise not to waste my time or a visitor's. I just have a lot of interests . . . like in no particular order: 

- soccer/football (St. Louis CITY)
- baseball (beloved St. Louis Cardinals)
- music (jazz, Tool, Radiohead, Tom Waits, and on and on)
- books
- movies and TV
- science
- dogs
- food
- art
- tech
- medical/health

I will stay away from politics and cultural bullshit because I want this to be a happy place, a safe place, not a doomscrolling crack dungeon (nod to the Dungeon Crawler).

This is for me.
