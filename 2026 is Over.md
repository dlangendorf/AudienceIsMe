# Season is Over

I should have said this on Sunday, the 23rd: The 2026 Cardinals season is over. The unexpected push for a wild card playoff spot went splat 🫟.

![[Pasted image 20260827104853.png|200]]
With recent pitching injuries, with the staff and bullpen wearing down, with thinning talent in the bullpen, the Cardinals crashed and burned -- and that's OK. It's supposed to be a rebuilding season, right?

St. Louis has talent -- Walker, Wetherholt, Baez, Herrera, Burleson, (maybe) Gorman -- but the pitching staff and bullpen are thin and are a mess no matter what Bernie says. Yeah, some of the metrics are positive, but on the whole the team keeps losing . . . 

So back to that rebuild.

Finish 2026 working with the young talent and prepare for 2027, if there is a season.
